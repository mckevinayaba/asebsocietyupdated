## LinkedIn post (Founder)

For five editions, since 2022, the ASEB Summit and Awards has brought together the people who build the rooms founders walk into: hub managers, programme leads, mentors, investors, policymakers and researchers. Every year the same question returned to us. What happens to all of that knowledge, all of those relationships, in the eleven months between summits?

ASEB Society is our answer, a permanent professional home for Africa's ecosystem builders, and asebsociety.org is where that home now lives. Practitioners can build a professional profile, share what they know through ASEB Voices and the Playbook, ask the village for help, and apply for membership. Institutions that want to strengthen the foundations of African entrepreneurship can start a conversation with us directly.

Much of this is still being built, and the site says plainly what is operational and what is not. That honesty matters to us, because a field that cannot tell the truth about its own capacity cannot build trust with anyone else.

It Takes a Village. Come and help us build it. www.asebsociety.org
