# Institutional blueprint

## Identity

ASEB Society advances ecosystem building as a professional practice and a recognised profession, strengthening the people, institutions and systems that make entrepreneurship possible across Africa. The Founder is Founder & Chief Executive Officer.

It is not primarily a conference, an awards organisation, an accelerator, a networking site, a training provider or a conventional NGO. It is permanent professional and institutional infrastructure.

- Tagline: It Takes a Village.
- Promise: Strengthening those who strengthen entrepreneurship.
- Primary positioning: Stronger ecosystems. Stronger economies.

## The problem

Entrepreneurs depend on support systems: hubs, incubators, accelerators, universities, investors, mentors, agencies, policymakers, researchers and enterprise development practitioners. Those systems are only as effective as the people who design and run them. The Society's intervention focus covers fragmented support systems, weak coordination, limited professional development, institutional fragility, uneven access to knowledge, limited institutional memory, geographic inequality, inadequate recognition, insufficient collective representation, weak translation of research into practice and limited collaboration across national ecosystems.

These are presented as problem areas and intervention focus. They are never claimed to hold equally in every country, and the Society never claims to solve unemployment by itself.

## Theory of change

Institutional investment strengthens the Society. The Society strengthens ecosystem builders through development, knowledge, coordination, recognition and collaboration. Stronger builders contribute to stronger support institutions. Stronger institutions contribute to better conditions for entrepreneurs. Better conditions can contribute to enterprise development, productivity, employment and transformation.

Each step is an intended outcome. Measured impact is reported separately, and only when evidence exists.

## Public narrative (Founding Organisational Profile 2026/2027)

- **Proposition.** Building the field behind the ecosystem. ASEB Society advances the professional field responsible for building entrepreneurial ecosystems: professional identity, shared knowledge, capabilities, methods, ethics, accountability, memory, learning, recognition, continuity and practitioner sustainability. It is not an accelerator, marketplace, conference organiser, general network or umbrella authority, and it never claims to be the only institution doing relevant work.
- **Three levels of intervention.** Entrepreneurship development (building entrepreneurs), ecosystem development (building ecosystems), professional field development (building the field of ecosystem building). They overlap and reinforce one another. The Society's distinctive focus is the third.
- **Narrative independence.** The case rests on the Society's own founding purpose, dating from 2022, and on the structural needs of the field. No public page names, compares with or cites the closure of another organisation to justify the Society. The internal profile's landscape chapter and the profile itself are never published. A public edition of the profile may be published later, once approved, without competitor references, confidential evaluations or unapproved governance, and no download link appears until that file exists.
- **Chronology.** 2022 Johannesburg, the Summit & Awards established. 2023 Nairobi, conversations about a permanent organisation began. 2024 Accra, they deepened. 2025 Addis Ababa, the intention to establish the Society was announced, with early mobilisation in Ethiopia that is not an appointment. 21 October 2026 Johannesburg, ASEB Society officially launched. The Society is new. The work, relationships and experiences that gave birth to it are not.
- **Summit and Society.** The Summit brings the field together. The Society sustains the work throughout the year, through the cycle Listen, Develop, Convene, Follow through.
- **Funding.** Fund the infrastructure, not merely the activity. Six proposed funding areas, none costed or committed. Institutional funding is distinct from Summit sponsorship.
- **Standing.** Registered participant, membership applicant, approved member, contributor, recognised ecosystem builder, verified practitioner and accredited professional are distinct. No accreditation, certification or designation is offered.
- **Historical figures** (5 editions, 12,700+ nominations, 245+ recognised, 1,786 nominations from 52 countries in 2026) belong to the Summit & Awards, carry their reconciliation note, and are never Society membership, representation or impact.

## Governance

One institution. One governing Board. Four institutional pillars. Clear executive accountability. Specialist support where necessary. Independent awards assessment. The ASEB Society Board governs every pillar, the Summit & Awards included. The Summit & Awards is the flagship continental convening and recognition programme of ASEB Society, not a separately governed institution. Award recipients are selected by an Independent Awards Jury, which is not a governing body. There is no Summit Advisory Board, no three layer model and no Advisory Council. See `docs/GOVERNANCE.md`.
