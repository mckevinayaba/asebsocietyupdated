# Editorial and moderation policy (draft for review)

**Status:** draft for Secretariat approval. It describes how the tools in `/editorial` should be used. The tools exist. The policy that governs them is not yet approved.

## Principles

Accuracy before speed. Every claim about people, institutions, numbers or impact must be verifiable. Planned work is never presented as delivered. ASEB Voices is the single storytelling brand, and Founders' Voices is one of its categories.

## Roles

Moderators handle community posts, questions, answers and reports. Editors handle stories, Playbook contributions, research and opportunities, and may schedule and feature. Secretariat administrators decide when community publishing opens. Roles are held only by verified accounts and are granted in the database by the project owner.

## Decisions

| Decision | When | What the contributor sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publish | Meets the guidelines and is accurate as far as can be checked | Published, with their chosen name |
| Schedule | Approved for a later date | Scheduled, then published |
| Decline | Off topic, unverifiable, promotional, or breaches the guidelines | Declined, with a reason |
| Hide | Already published content that later breaches the guidelines or receives a valid report | Hidden, with a reason |

The platform has no "request changes" state today, so a fixable submission is declined with a reason and the contributor may resubmit. Every decision should carry a short reason in the notes field. Reports should be reviewed within [confirm, for example 3 working days].

## Corrections and complaints

Factual errors are corrected openly with a dated note. Complaints go to aseb@asebsociety.org and are acknowledged within [confirm].

## Community publishing mode

Publishing starts pre-moderated. It should not be opened until the Privacy Notice, Terms of Use, Contributor Terms and this policy are approved and published, and at least one moderator besides the Founder is provisioned.
