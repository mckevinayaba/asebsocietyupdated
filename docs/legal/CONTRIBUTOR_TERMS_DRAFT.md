# Contributor terms (draft for review)

**Status:** draft. Not approved, not published, not legal advice. The platform already records the two permissions described below with each submission.

1. **You keep ownership.** You retain copyright in what you contribute to The Field, ASEB Voices, the Playbook, Ask the Village and State of the Field.
2. **Permission to publish.** By ticking permission to publish, you grant the Society a non exclusive, royalty free licence to publish, display and archive your contribution on the platform and in the Society's own communications about it, with attribution to the name you chose.
3. **Permission to edit.** Editing is a separate permission. Without it, the Society publishes your words as submitted or declines them. With it, editors may make light changes for clarity, length and accuracy, and will not change your meaning.
4. **What you confirm.** That the contribution is your own or that you have the right to share it, that you have consent from anyone identifiable in it (including in photographs), and that it does not disclose confidential information.
5. **Review.** Contributions are reviewed before publication. The Society may decline any contribution and will say so in your account.
6. **Withdrawal.** You can withdraw a contribution at any time from your account. Withdrawal removes it from the platform. Copies already shared by others outside the platform cannot be recalled. [confirm response time for withdrawal requests made by email]
7. **Corrections.** If a published contribution contains an error of fact, the Society may add a correction notice.
